# Twine Security

> Twine Security is a cybersecurity company that builds AI Digital Employees to help cyber teams close the industry's critical talent gap. Founded by cybersecurity experts from Claroty, Twine creates trustworthy Agentic AI professionals who execute security tasks from A to Z, enabling cybersecurity teams to focus on strategic initiatives.

## About This Site

This website provides information about Twine's AI Digital Employees, their capabilities, and how they augment cybersecurity team performance. Twine's first digital employee, Alex, specializes in Identity and Access Management (IAM). Last updated: November 2025
